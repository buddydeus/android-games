## Layout

Square `1254 × 1254` design preview with a centered circular-safe medallion. The production package copy is resized to `1024 × 1024` without cropping.

## Modules

- Cool mineral-grey double porcelain outer rim.
- Ivory inner board plate with thin warm-gold grid incisions.
- One glossy S-shaped mineral-blue river.
- Upper-left pine-green embossed square `象` piece.
- Lower-right cinnabar embossed square `鼠` piece.
- Transparent exterior beyond the circular medallion.

## Visual details

Preserve the user-selected artwork rather than regenerating or reinterpreting it. The two square pieces retain their exact traditional Chinese glyphs, cream lettering, gold inset rims, glazing, bevel, and shallow contact shadows. The alpha edge is clean and circular-safe with no white or chroma halo.
